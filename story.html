Our story

Our mission

The Cap Bar is a family oriented custom hat experience where creativity, individuality, and community come together under one roof.

We design custom hats for both adults and children, giving customers the opportunity to create something on their own. From choosing their personal unique style and colors to selecting personalized details and finishing touches. Every customer gets to bring their creative vision to life on the spot.

Our goal is to be more than a retail store. We want The Cap Bar to feel like a place where everyone is welcomed and instantly feels like family. Whether customers are shopping for themselves, creating matching family hats, celebrating a special occasion, or simply looking for a fun and creative experience.

THE CAP BAR IS MORE THAN JUST A HAT SHOP — IT'S AN EXPERIENCE!
