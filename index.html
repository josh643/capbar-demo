How it works

The Cap Bar experience

Every logo pattern is designed by hand ahead of time, so when you walk in, your hat is pressed and ready in under 10 minutes.

  1. 1

    Pick your cap

    Trucker or snapback, in pink, blue or black. Try a few on, the fit matters.

  2. 2

    Choose your design

    Pick one of our signature logo patterns or bring your own idea and we'll design it with you.

  3. 3

    Ready in 10 minutes

    We press it at the customization station while you wait. You walk out wearing it.
